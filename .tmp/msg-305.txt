fix(cosem)!: correct PRIME PLC MAC setup class_id from 80 to 82

IEC 62056-6-2 ED4 (2021) §4.12.6 and DLMS UA Blue Book Ed.
12.1 §4.12.6 both define "PRIME NB OFDM PLC MAC setup" with
class_id = 82, not 80. Class_id 80 is reserved by the spec
for the unrelated "61334-4-32 LLC SSCS setup" IC.

Our built-in `CosemPrimePlcMacSetupObject` carried the
correct attribute layout but reported the wrong class_id on
the wire (`80` instead of `82`), which would not interoperate
with a spec-conformant client/server.

Change `kPrimePlcMacSetupClassId` from 80 to 82, refresh the
unit-test class_id check, the COSEM IC support matrix and
the COSEM API guide. The support matrix now lists IC `80`
separately as application-provided.

Tests: dlms_cosem_tests 277/277, full ctest 938/938.

BREAKING CHANGE: `CosemPrimePlcMacSetupObject` instances now
report class_id `82` instead of `80` in their descriptor and
on the wire. Clients/servers that previously interoperated
with the incorrect class_id `80` will need to be updated to
the spec-conformant id `82`.
